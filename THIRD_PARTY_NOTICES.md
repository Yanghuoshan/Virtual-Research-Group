# Third-Party Content and Provenance

## Source and Adaptation

Reused material originated from `../.claude/skills/` and https://github.com/Orchestra-Research/AI-research-SKILLs . The original directory remains unchanged. Its 417-file baseline is retained in `source-snapshot.json`; the installed source does not identify a verifiable upstream commit.

The former six knowledge packages now live in the flat `skills/` namespace. Their entry documents have been rewritten as bounded specialists: hypothesis ranking, problem reformulation, ML writing, systems writing, quantitative plotting and talk composition. This is a structural adaptation, not a claim of byte-identical upstream skills.

Citation API references were moved to `citation-verification`; diagram references moved to `research-diagram-design`. Cross-skill redirection and model-selection instructions in affected references were removed or bounded to core-assigned tools. Earlier English-language adaptations remain, including the AAAI template README's existing English section.

`provenance.json` maps every reused source file to its current path and records original and current hashes with adaptation notes. It is an integrity and attribution record, not a routing registry. New first-party specialist entries do not need registration there. Preserved templates, reference text and binary examples retain their original notices.

## Licensing

The source skill metadata declared MIT, but the locally installed source lacks the full upstream license. LaTeX templates include separate authors and licenses, including LPPL material whose distribution conditions may require companion source files not present here.

Do not treat all assets as MIT or the bundle as cleared for public redistribution. Before publishing, obtain the corresponding upstream licenses, verify each template and asset's terms, retain notices, and supply required source material or omit those assets and link to their source. Entry rewriting does not remove attribution obligations.

## Applicability and Execution

Retained historical API examples, dates, venue requirements and quantitative claims require independent verification. No template was compiled and no research example was run as part of adapting these documents. Reference examples do not authorize installation, external services, model selection, scheduling, submissions or extra research. Specialist execution is bounded by the core's explicit assignment.
