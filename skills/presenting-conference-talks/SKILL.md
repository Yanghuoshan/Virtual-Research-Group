# Research Talk Composition

Convert a supplied research narrative into a talk for a specified audience. Adapted from Orchestra Research's conference-talk guidance and its attribution to ARIS and Mike Dahlin. This skill does not produce new research evidence or manage other content producers.

## Inputs

An approved manuscript, verified figures, permitted claims, audience background, talk duration, desired output format, available rendering tools, and assigned paths.

## Method

1. Identify one takeaway, the problem it addresses, the mechanism, and the strongest supplied evidence. Distinguish the paper's full contribution from what fits the talk.
2. Allocate time to motivation, approach, evidence, limits, and conclusion. Start near one slide per minute for an oral talk, then adjust to density and rehearsal rather than treating that ratio as a rule.
3. Give each slide one message. Use supplied figures with readable labels; explain the comparison and uncertainty rather than copying paragraphs.
4. Introduce notation before using it. For systems material, walk through a concrete request. For theoretical material, present assumptions and an intuition before the formal statement.
5. Write notes with timing, the point to convey, and the transition. Put supporting details in backup slides; do not hide critical limitations there.
6. Use the assigned Beamer or presentation toolchain only when available and permitted. Otherwise return an outline or source with its rendering blocker. Do not start live services or experiments to create a demonstration.
7. Check total duration, source fidelity, readability, and consistency between slide text and notes.

## Outputs

The requested slide outline or rendered/source artifact, timed speaker notes, figure references, and any rendering or evidence limitations at the assigned paths.

## Checks

No headline claim exceeds the approved manuscript. All numbers and figures retain provenance. Slides remain legible at presentation size. Rehearsal estimates and actual timings are distinguished.

Example: a five-minute talk should emphasize one mechanism and one decisive comparison rather than compress twenty pages into tiny slides.

## Boundary

Return to the core with the artifact or blockers. Do not dispatch other skills or agents, select models, commission figures, run demos, schedule rehearsals, advance research phases, or modify global state.
