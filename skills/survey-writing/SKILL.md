# Survey Manuscript Writing

Compose a survey from verified literature artifacts into a review manuscript. This skill writes from supplied, verified material; it never broadens the search, verifies new citations on its own authority, or claims coverage beyond the recorded screening ledger.

## Inputs

The verified literature artifacts (screening ledger, evidence tables, verified bibliography), the survey's scope and audience, the target venue or format constraints, current findings, and assigned output paths under `paper/`. Unverified citations or an unfinished screening ledger are blockers, not material to silently absorb.

## Method

1. Restate the review scope from the supplied artifacts: question, inclusion criteria, and the coverage limits the ledger actually supports, following [survey structure](references/survey-structure.md).
2. Choose a thematic or method-driven organization that the evidence table supports; every section must map to rows that exist in the table, not to themes the authors wish existed.
3. Draft each section citing only the verified bibliography; every factual statement about a cited work carries its source locator. Claims beyond the ledger are marked as coverage limitations, not asserted.
4. Build the comparison tables from the evidence tables without reinterpreting the entries; disagreements between studies are reported, not averaged.
5. Write the coverage and limitations section from the ledger counts: what was searched, what was excluded and why, and what the survey cannot conclude.
6. Return the draft with a claim-to-source map and the list of gaps found while writing.

## Outputs

At the assigned path: the survey manuscript draft, comparison tables, a claim-to-source map, and a gap list for the core. The draft is a proposal; publication decisions belong to the core.

## Checks

Every citation appears in the verified bibliography; every characterization of a cited work traces to its recorded locator; coverage claims match ledger counts; no theme section exists without supporting table rows.

Example: a ledger of forty screened studies supports "a structured view of forty studies", not "a comprehensive survey of the field".

## Boundary

Return to the core with the draft, tables, or exact blockers. Do not dispatch other skills or agents, select models, retrieve or verify new citations, broaden the search, update global findings/state, or advance phases. Submitting the manuscript is a separate core decision.
