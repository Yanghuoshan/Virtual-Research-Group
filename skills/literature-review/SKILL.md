# Bounded Literature Search and Synthesis

Review a defined body of literature, not an open-ended research agenda. Distinguish a search plan, a supplied-corpus review, and an executed systematic search; none implies exhaustive coverage.

## Inputs

Question, population or task, comparison and outcomes of interest, date/language/source limits, review type, eligibility criteria, supplied papers or source access, assigned tools and budget, output paths, and acceptance criteria. Request missing scope decisions from the core. With no papers or retrieval permission, return only an explicitly unexecuted search plan.

## Method

1. Make the search reproducible: map concepts to synonyms and Boolean queries, identify permitted databases and time limits, and state inclusion/exclusion rules before screening, following [search planning](references/search-planning.md). Record any later changes with reasons; do not broaden the assigned question.
2. Use only core-assigned servers/tools, with the external access the core approved and named in the task objective, for retrieval. Record exact query, source, retrieval date, filters, pagination/limits, and raw responses. A failed or truncated search is a coverage limitation, not zero results. Without access, work from the supplied corpus or report a blocker.
3. Normalize identifiers and deduplicate records; link preprints, revisions and published versions of the same study. Track screening at title/abstract and full-text levels with per-record decisions and exclusion reasons, using the [screening ledger](references/screening-ledger.md). Report observed PRISMA-style counts only; label unavailable full texts and unknown counts explicitly.
4. Extract an evidence table following the [extraction schema](references/extraction-and-reporting.md): study identity/version, source locator (page, section or table), population/data, design, comparison, outcome definition, effect and uncertainty, and limitations. Distinguish author-reported findings from reviewer interpretation. Mark missing information rather than reconstructing it from memory.
5. Assess risk of bias domain by domain with the [bias assessment](references/bias-assessment.md) scale: selection, confounding, leakage, missing outcomes, selective reporting, and unequal baseline budgets. Shared datasets or duplicate reports are not independent confirmations. Venue reputation and citation count do not establish evidence quality.
6. Synthesize by claim, mechanism and study conditions, including contradictory and null findings, under the [synthesis rules](references/extraction-and-reporting.md). Pool estimates only when the assignment and compatible estimands, units and uncertainty support it; otherwise provide narrative synthesis and explain heterogeneity. Separate lack of evidence from evidence of no effect. For opportunity mapping, also record per claim the load-bearing assumption, the boundary or condition left untested, and any contradiction that no ordinary confound explains. This is an evidence map for later question selection, not a contribution claim or a novelty verdict.

## Outputs

At new assigned paths: search plan/log and raw retrieval records when applicable; deduplication and screening ledger; study/evidence table with source locators; bounded synthesis, disagreements, coverage limits and unresolved gaps; and an opportunity-oriented summary of contradictions, hidden assumptions and untested boundaries, marked as evidence rather than as established novelty. Do not present a search plan as executed work. These outputs feed the opportunity map described in the framework reference `references/idea-exploration.md`.

## Checks

Every substantive assertion is traceable; counts reconcile to actual records; inaccessible studies are not silently treated as negative evidence. Do not invent citations, screening counts or an exhaustive/systematic-search claim from a convenience sample. Identification of a paper alone does not validate its conclusions.

Example: twelve supplied abstracts support a limited evidence map, not a completed full-text systematic review or invented PRISMA flow.

## Boundary

Return to the core with artifacts or exact blockers. Do not dispatch other skills or agents, select models or providers, acquire access, schedule searches, run experiments, update global findings/state, or advance phases. Search expansion and acceptance of research conclusions remain core decisions.
